## Understanding Volcano Jobs

### Job Structure

```yaml
apiVersion: batch.volcano.sh/v1beta1
kind: Job
spec:
  schedulerName: volcano
  tasks:
    - name: worker
      replicas: 4
      template:
        spec:
          containers:
            - name: worker
              resources:
                requests:
                  cpu: "4"
                  memory: "8Gi"
  maxRetry: 3              # Max retries before job is Aborted
  policies:
    - event: PodFailed
      action: RestartJob
```
